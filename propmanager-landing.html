Everything you need

Built for HMO
operators.

Every feature designed around the real workflows of rent-to-rent operators managing multiple properties across South London and beyond.

💷
Smart Rent Collection
Automatic weekly and monthly schedules per tenant. Due Today, Tomorrow, Overdue and Collected tabs. Bulk WhatsApp chasing, late fee calculation, and bank vs cash breakdown — all on one page.
☁️
Cloud Sync via Supabase
All data synced in real time. Works across phone, tablet and desktop simultaneously. Room photos uploaded to Supabase Storage and served instantly from any device.
🏡
Available Rooms Marketing
Photo gallery per room, description editor, Google Maps link. Share a formatted room listing — price, postcode, photos — directly to WhatsApp in one tap.
🔑
Tenant Portal Credentials
Auto-generated username and password for every tenant — no email required. Regenerate in one click. Share login details directly via WhatsApp so tenants can access their portal instantly.
📊
Financial Dashboard
Live P&L by month — income, landlord costs, expenses and net profit. Loss-making properties surface automatically. YTD and monthly views with drill-down.
🏦
Landlord Payments
Track guaranteed rent to each landlord. Mark payments as paid, see overdue amounts, manage bank details, and view full payment history per property.
🛠️
Maintenance Tracker
Log issues with photos, category and priority. Assign to properties and rooms. Track open vs resolved. Urgent items surface at the top of your dashboard automatically.
📄
Tenancy Agreements
Generate a print-ready AST for any tenant in seconds. Pre-filled with their details, rent, pay day and deposit. Export as PDF or print directly from the browser.
🔒
Role-Based Access
Admin, Manager, Maintenance and Viewer roles. Each team member sees only what they need. Switch between users instantly without losing data.
