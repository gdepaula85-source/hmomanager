*** Opening paragraph. Write it like a newspaper lede — who is this for, what problem does it solve, and why should they keep reading. Keep under 60 words. ***

Use H2 for the main sections of the post

Write paragraphs like a human. Short sentences. Strong opinions. Specific numbers. Avoid "In today's fast-paced world" and every AI-slop opener. Readers can smell it.

When referencing features, link directly — e.g. check our HMO landlord tools or Service Accommodation module. Inbound links between blog posts and operator pages help SEO.

Use H3 for subsections inside a section

You can use bold for emphasis inside a paragraph, but sparingly. inline code works if you're writing anything technical.

  • Bullet lists break up dense text — great for feature enumerations or steps.
  • Keep each bullet one line where possible. Two at most.
  • Three to seven bullets reads best; longer lists become noise.
Pull-quotes are for the single best line of the post. Use them once, sparingly. They work well under a heading to set a section's tone.

A good post ends with a clear takeaway

Close with the one thing you want the reader to do or believe. If they only skim, the closing paragraph is what they'll actually read.
