Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Who we are

LandlordApp.io is registered in England and Wales. We are the data controller for the personal data we collect from you.

2. Data we collect

Account data: name, email, password (hashed), organisation name. Portfolio data: properties, tenants, rent records, compliance documents, maintenance logs — all uploaded by you. Usage data: pages visited, features used, IP address for security purposes.

3. How we use it

To deliver the Service, process payments (via Stripe), send transactional emails (via Resend), provide AI insights (via Anthropic Claude API). We never sell your data to third parties.

4. Data storage

Data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) infrastructure hosted within the EU. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Row-level security ensures one organisation cannot access another's data.

5. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data. Email admin@landlordapp.io to make a request — we respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies only — for login sessions and preferences. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

7. Third-party processors

Stripe (payments), Resend (email), Supabase (database), Anthropic (AI). Each is GDPR-compliant.

8. Contact

Data questions: admin@landlordapp.io.
